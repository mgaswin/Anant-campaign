The English translation, The Layered Bride Collection, serves as the sub-brand for pan-India retail and digital contexts.

Positioning Statement
"For the modern Indian bride who refuses to stop at one moment, Ek Se Anant is the only bridal jewellery collection designed to multiply — in style, in occasion, in meaning — so her gold is as dynamic as she is."

Brand Story & Emotional Hook
She Didn't Inherit One Story.
There was a time when a bride's jewellery was locked in a velvet box after the wedding — too precious for Tuesday, too heavy for brunch, too specific for anything but that single sacred day. But she is not a single day. She is every morning she wakes up and chooses who she wants to be.
"Ek Se Anant was born from a simple truth: the woman who chose each other across a sacred fire also chose herself — every single day after."
The collection is a celebration of her plurality. The bride who dances at the sangeet and pitches to investors on Monday. The woman who wears her gold to a gala and to a girls' lunch. Each necklace layer is a chapter. Worn together, they tell her whole story. Worn alone, each speaks for a moment she is living right now.
Core Consumer Insight
The Tension We're Unlocking

"I spent more on my bridal jewellery than my first car. I've worn it exactly once."

The modern urban Indian bride (24–35, upper-middle-class, social-media-fluent) experiences deep cognitive dissonance around bridal jewellery. She wants heirloom-quality gold but resents occasion-locked pieces. She is influenced by global styling trends and sees layered necklaces on every fashion feed she consumes. She wants to invest in jewellery that justifies itself over time — not just on her wedding day. Her deepest desire: jewellery that mirrors her identity — complex, versatile, and never finished.

Functional Desire

Jewellery that serves her across ethnic, fusion, and western outfits. Pieces that stack for occasions and strip down for everyday luxury.

Emotional Desire

To feel like her jewellery belongs to her story — not just to one day. To show the world she is not defined by a single moment.

Core Campaign Idea
The #LayerHerStory Campaign
One necklace is a beginning. Multiple layers are a biography. The campaign invites real and aspirational Indian women to share the stories behind their layers — what each piece represents, which occasion it was worn for, which mood it matched. It turns jewellery into autobiography.
Campaign Concept Film — "The Chapters"

A 90-second brand film told in 5 chapters

We follow one woman — a bride on her wedding morning. Each subsequent chapter skips forward: the honeymoon dinner (one layer, effortless), the festive gathering (two layers, celebratory), the work event (a single delicate piece, powerful), the girls' night (full stack, joyful). The final frame: her daughter trying on the same piece years later. Voiceover: "Some jewellery marks a day. Ek Se Anant marks a life."

Influencer & Creator Strategy
A Layered Influencer Architecture
We deploy a three-tier influencer strategy that mirrors the product's own layering philosophy — macro anchors, mid-tier amplifiers, and micro-layer authenticity creators.
01
Tier 1 — The Brand Faces (2–3 faces)

Bollywood or regional celebrity brides who have recently married and are culturally relevant — e.g. a southern actress, a Hindi film star, a fashion designer. They wear Ek Se Anant at real public appearances, not just campaigns. This drives earned media and creates genuine celebrity association. Budget: ₹1.5–3 Cr per face for a 12-month contract.

02
Tier 2 — The Style Amplifiers (15–20 creators)

Fashion and bridal influencers with 300K–3M followers across Instagram and YouTube. Bridalwear stylists, fashion bloggers, and bridal content creators. They create "style with me" and "how I styled my Ek Se Anant" content. Paid partnerships with creative freedom. 60-day exclusivity in bridal jewellery category. Budget: ₹8–25L per creator.

03
Tier 3 — The Real Brides Layer (50–100 micro creators)

Real brides or newlywed women with 10K–150K followers. Authentic, trusted, and highly engaged. Gifting program — real brides receive a layer necklace and share their story organically. UGC is then amplified via Malabar's owned channels. Low cost, highest authenticity ROI.

Special Collaboration Formats

The Designer Collab

Co-create a limited edition "layer" with a celebrated Indian fashion designer (Sabyasachi, Tarun Tahiliani, or an emerging name). The limited run creates premium buzz and fashion press coverage. This elevates the product from jewellery to fashion object.

The Stylist Partnership

Partner with 3–5 celebrity stylists who work with brides and Bollywood. They recommend Ek Se Anant to clients and create co-branded styling guides. Their audience of fashion-forward women is exactly the target consumer.

Creator Content Brief Framework

Every influencer brief includes these non-negotiables:

  • Show the necklace in minimum 2 different styling contexts (e.g. with saree + with western outfit)
  • Demonstrate the modular layering feature — assemble or disassemble on camera
  • Mention price range and "starts from ₹X" to set accessibility expectations
  • Include call to action: "Link in bio" or "Visit Malabar Gold nearest to you"
  • Use brand hashtags: #EkSeAnant #LayerHerStory #MalabarBride
In-Store Experience & Visual Merchandising
The Layering Studio
Transform the Ek Se Anant in-store experience into a destination — not just a counter, but a private jewellery studio experience that mirrors the premium, personalised ethos of the brand.
In-Store Experience Design

The Layering Alcove

A dedicated, ring-lit styling corner within flagship Malabar stores. Features: a full-length mirror with soft editorial lighting, a curated display of all Ek Se Anant layers in ascending weight, a styled "inspiration wall" showing celebrity and real-bride looks, and seating for a personal styling consultation. The space is photographable by design — encouraging in-store Instagram moments.

Visual Merchandising

  • Modular display stands that physically show layers "building up" — from one piece to five
  • Weight callouts on each display tag (16g starter → 46g full stack)
  • QR codes linking to styling videos for each piece
  • Campaign imagery on large-format prints: editorial, aspirational, modern bride
  • Co-ordinated packaging: black box with gold foil, each layer in its own compartment

Bridal Styling Consultations

  • "Layer Your Look" appointment — 45-min private session with a trained bridal stylist
  • Bride brings her lehenga/saree fabric swatch and the stylist builds a personalised stack
  • Digital lookbook emailed post-appointment with all combinations tried
  • Complimentary champagne/welcome drink — creates an event feeling
  • Consultation drives multi-piece purchase conversion significantly

Launch Campaign Roadmap
Three Phases. One Story.
Phase 1 · Months 1–2
The Whisper — Pre-Launch
Build desire before the reveal. Create anticipation among the target audience.
  • Teaser campaign on Instagram: silhouette imagery of layered necklaces with the line "Something is about to layer up."
  • Seed Tier 1 influencers with the collection under NDA — they wear it at real events to spark curiosity
  • Press briefings with top fashion editors for a coordinated launch story
  • Internal training: all Malabar store staff trained on the product concept, layering philosophy, and styling guidance
  • Build the Layering Studio alcoves in 10 flagship stores across metros
  • Develop the microsite and Layer Builder digital tool
  • Identify and confirm 50 micro-bride creators for gifting program
Phase 2 · Month 3 (Wedding Season Timing)
The Reveal — Launch Week
A coordinated, high-visibility launch that creates cultural momentum.
  • Day 1 — The Film Drop: 90-sec brand film "The Chapters" released across YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook simultaneously. All Tier 1 influencers share on the same day
  • Day 1 — Press Coverage: Coordinated editorial in Vogue India, Harper's Bazaar India, leading wedding publications
  • Day 2 — The Event: A launch event in Mumbai (flagship city) — intimate press + influencer dinner at a premium venue. Real-time Instagram coverage
  • Day 3–7: Tier 2 influencer content drops — "unboxing + style with me" videos cascade across the week
  • Week 1: Go live with Meta and Google paid campaigns. Activate Pinterest promoted pins. Launch microsite and Layer Builder tool
  • Week 2: First wave of micro-bride UGC seeded — reshare on Malabar's channels
Phase 3 · Months 4–12
The Layer — Sustain & Deepen
Build the collection into a perennial category. Sustain relevance across the full year.
  • Months 4–6: "Her Layers" YouTube docuseries — one real bride per episode, released bi-weekly. Deep engagement content
  • Month 5: Bridal trunk shows across 6 cities — Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi
  • Diwali window (Month 6–7): Festive gifting campaign — "Layer Her with Love." Position individual necklaces as festival gifts
  • Month 7: Limited edition designer collab drop — creates fresh press cycle and premium halo
  • Month 8: Wedding expo season — presence at top bridal fairs nationally
  • Year-end: Anniversary campaign — "365 Days. Infinite Layers." UGC celebration of one year of the collection. Community contest: share how many occasions you've worn your stack
Cross-Selling & Upselling Opportunities
The Stack Economy

The Starter Stack

Entry purchase of 1 layer (16g). Positioned as the bridal gift or self-purchase. In-store consultation reveals complementary layers. Follow-up within 60 days: "Complete your stack" push notification / email.

Anniversary Add-On

CRM trigger at 6-month and 1-year anniversaries. "Add a new chapter to her stack." Personalised recommendations based on what she already owns. Creates repeat purchase behavior with emotional framing.

The Complete Story

Position the full 3–5 layer set (approaching 46g) as the aspirational "complete collection." Bridal package pricing that bundles layers at a compelling value vs. single purchases. In-store display always shows the full stack.

Cross-Category Opportunities

Natural cross-sell into matching earrings designed to complement the layered necklace aesthetic; a coordinated bracelet stack collection that mirrors the necklace layering concept; and a "The Complete Layer Look" — necklace + earrings + bracelet bundle for brides who want a complete modular trousseau.

Customer Engagement Strategy
The Ek Se Anant Community

The Layered Brides Club

A CRM loyalty program specific to Ek Se Anant buyers. Members receive early access to new layers, invite-only trunk show invitations, personal styling calls, and "stack anniversary" reminders. Creates a community of brand ambassadors who feel seen and valued.

#LayerHerStory UGC Engine

Monthly UGC contests — "Show us your layer this month." Winners featured on Malabar's national Instagram. Monthly theme: January = Festive, February = Anniversary, March = Work Luxe. Keeps community engaged year-round with fresh, authentic content.
